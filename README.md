###  Overview
**Stranger Things** is a *sci-fi horror drama* series created by **The Duffer Brothers** and released on **Netflix**.  
Set in the **1980s** in Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of kids who uncover supernatural secrets and government experiments involving a mysterious girl with telekinetic powers.
